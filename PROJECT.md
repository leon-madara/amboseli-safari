# Amboseli Safari Club - Website Project

## Project Overview

This is a **pitch website** designed to win a client deal for Amboseli Safari Club, a new **modern safari hotel** opening in December 2025 near Kimana Gate, Amboseli, Kenya. The website demonstrates technical capabilities while serving as a pre-launch marketing platform strategically positioned for the family and group safari market.

**Project Status:** Pre-Launch Marketing Site
**Target Launch Date:** December 2025
**Current Purpose:** Client Pitch & Early Bookings
**Tech Stack:** Next.js, TypeScript, Framer Motion, CSS Modules
**Market Position:** Modern Safari Hotel ($200-450/night) - Family & Group Focused

**IMPORTANT:** See [STRATEGIC-POSITIONING.md](STRATEGIC-POSITIONING.md) for detailed market analysis and honest positioning strategy.

## Business Context

### The Client
**Amboseli Safari Club** is a new modern safari hotel located near Kimana Gate of Amboseli National Park in Kenya (Kajiado County). The property features multi-story accommodation buildings, a large swimming pool, and contemporary amenities - positioned to serve families, groups, and domestic travelers seeking authentic safari experiences with modern comfort.

**Property Type:** Modern Safari Hotel (not luxury tented camp)
**Location:** Adjacent to Kimana Gate, Amboseli National Park
**Distance from Nairobi:** 3-4 hours (365km southeast)
**Unique Feature:** Legitimate Mount Kilimanjaro views from property

### Project Goals

1. **Win the Client Deal** - Demonstrate strategic thinking AND technical excellence
2. **Honest Market Positioning** - Position as modern safari hotel serving families/groups (not competing with $1000/night luxury camps)
3. **Drive Early Bookings** - Target 50+ reservations with 20% early bird discount
4. **Build Sustainable Business** - Focus on achievable 60% occupancy in underserved market segment
5. **Attract Top Talent** - Recruit hospitality professionals for December 2025 opening
6. **Showcase Technical Excellence** - Innovative scroll-driven storytelling with realistic content

### Strategic Approach

**The Honest Positioning:**
Rather than competing with ultra-luxury tented camps ($800-1500/night), Amboseli Safari Club fills a critical market gap as a **modern safari hotel** serving families, groups, tour operators, and domestic travelers at $200-450/night - a segment currently underserved in Amboseli.

**Why This Wins:**
- Targets the RIGHT customers (who will love what you offer)
- Builds 5-star reviews (exceeds expectations vs disappoints)
- Generates consistent bookings (larger market segment)
- Demonstrates strategic business thinking to client

## Website Strategy

### Design Philosophy

This website uses **cinematic scroll-driven storytelling** to take visitors on a 24-hour journey through a day at Amboseli Safari Club. The approach:

1. **Immersive Experience** - 12 chapters representing different times of day
2. **Emotional Engagement** - Atmospheric effects, parallax animations, time-based gradients
3. **Progressive Disclosure** - Content reveals naturally as users scroll
4. **Conversion-Focused** - Strategic CTAs throughout the journey
5. **Performance-Optimized** - Code splitting, lazy loading, optimized animations

### The 12-Chapter Journey

| Chapter | Title | Height | Purpose |
|---------|-------|--------|---------|
| 1 | Pre-Dawn Hero | 100vh | First impression, countdown timer, primary CTA |
| 2 | Sunrise | 120vh | Vision statement, opening announcement |
| 3 | Morning Drive | 150vh | Wildlife showcase, location context |
| 4 | Bush Breakfast | 150vh | Signature dining experiences |
| 5 | Accommodations | 180vh | Room types, early bird pricing |
| 6 | Dining Experience | 150vh | All dining venues and experiences |
| 7 | Safari Experiences | 220vh | Curated adventures, expert guides |
| 8 | Wellness | 120vh | Spa, fitness, pool amenities |
| 9 | Guest Stories | 200vh | Social proof, waitlist signup |
| 10 | Location & Access | 150vh | Proximity to park, Nairobi access |
| 11 | Work With Us | 100vh | Career opportunities, team values |
| 12 | Plan Your Safari | 180vh | Packages, pricing, contact, booking |

**Total Journey:** 1,820vh (18.2x viewport heights)

## Technical Architecture

### Tech Stack
- **Framework:** Next.js 14 (App Router)
- **Language:** TypeScript
- **Styling:** CSS Modules + CSS Variables
- **Animation:** Framer Motion
- **State Management:** React Hooks
- **Performance:** Dynamic imports, lazy loading
- **Deployment:** Vercel (recommended)

### Project Structure
```
amboseli-safari-club/
├── src/
│   ├── app/                      # Next.js app router
│   │   └── (marketing)/         # Marketing pages group
│   ├── components/
│   │   ├── atoms/               # Basic UI components
│   │   ├── molecules/           # Composite components
│   │   ├── organisms/           # Complex sections
│   │   └── chapters/            # 12 chapter components
│   ├── data/
│   │   ├── chapters.ts          # Chapter configurations
│   │   ├── openingInfo.ts       # Opening announcements
│   │   └── contact.ts           # Contact information
│   ├── styles/
│   │   └── variables.css        # Design tokens
│   └── types/
│       └── chapter.ts           # TypeScript definitions
├── public/
│   └── images/                  # Static assets
└── PROJECT.md                   # This file
```

### Key Files

#### Chapter Configuration
**File:** `src/data/chapters.ts`
Defines all 12 chapters with heights, start/end positions, components, and atmospheric effects.

#### Chapter Components
**Location:** `src/components/chapters/`
Each chapter is a self-contained component with its own styles and logic.

#### Design Tokens
**File:** `src/styles/variables.css`
Global CSS variables for colors, spacing, typography, and animations.
